# Metric Fastener Reference — Clearance Holes, Counterbores, Countersinks

Source: ISO 273 (clearance holes), ISO 4762 (socket head cap screws), ISO 10642 (flat head screws)

## M3 Fastener Reference
Thread: M3 × 0.5mm pitch, nominal diameter 3mm
Clearance hole (ISO 273 medium fit): 3.4mm diameter
Socket head counterbore (ISO 4762): 6.5mm diameter × 3.0mm deep
Flat head countersink (90°, ISO 10642): 6.72mm diameter
Minimum thread engagement (steel): 4.5mm | (aluminium): 6.0mm | (cast iron): 4.5mm
Standard edge distance from hole center: 8mm (minimum 5mm)
Minimum pitch (center-to-center): 9mm

## Bolt Circle (PCD) Standard Sizing for Flanges
4-bolt flanges: 4 holes at 90° spacing. First hole at (PCD/2, 0).
6-bolt flanges: 6 holes at 60° spacing. First hole at (PCD/2, 0).
8-bolt flanges: 8 holes at 45° spacing. First hole at (PCD/2, 0).
PCD selection guide: PCD ≈ 1.5 to 2.0 × flange OD for equal load distribution.
