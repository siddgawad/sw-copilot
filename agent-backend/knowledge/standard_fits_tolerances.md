# Standard Fits, Tolerances, and Geometric Dimensioning

Source: ISO 286 fits and tolerances system

## ISO Preferred Fits — Hole Basis System (ISO 286-1)

### Clearance Fits
H7/h6 — Sliding fit: shaft slides freely in hole, no perceptible play. Used for: precision sliding parts, spigots, guide pins.
H8/f7 — Running fit: shaft runs freely in hole. Used for: lightly loaded plain bearings, general shaft/hole assemblies.
H8/e7 — Easy running fit: noticeable clearance. Used for: journal bearings, loose running fits.
H11/c11 — Loose running fit: large clearance. Used for: wide commercial tolerances, large temperature variation.

### Transition Fits (may be clearance or interference)
H7/k6 — Push fit / locating fit: light interference or zero. Used for: locating dowels, spigots, pulley hubs. Can be assembled by hand or light mallet.
H7/js6 — Snug fit: equal chance of clearance or interference. Used for: close running fits, gear hubs.
H7/n6 — Press fit: definite interference. Used for: gears on shafts, coupling hubs (light press).

### Interference Fits
H7/p6 — Light press fit: requires press or thermal assistance. Used for: bushings in housings, permanent location fits.
H7/s6 — Medium drive fit: requires substantial press force. Used for: tight hubs, driven elements.
H7/u7 — Force fit: highest interference in standard system. Used for: permanent assembly, cannot be disassembled without damage.

## Dowel Pin Holes
Locating dowel diameter tolerance: H7 on hole, h6 on pin (transition fit H7/h6 → snug)
Standard dowel sizes: 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12mm diameter (ISO 8734)
Depth: minimum 1.5× diameter in each mating part; 2× diameter recommended
Edge distance: minimum 2× diameter from hole center to part edge

## Shaft Tolerances
General shaft h6: used with H7 hole for sliding/running fits
Bearing seat k6 (tight): 17mm bore k6 = +0.001 to +0.012mm (shaft oversize)
Ground shaft h5: tighter tolerance for precision running

## Geometric Tolerances (GD&T — ISO 1101)
Flatness general machined face: 0.05mm per 100mm
Parallelism of bolt-hole flanges: 0.1mm
Perpendicularity of boss to base: 0.05mm per 50mm height
Position of bolt holes (true position): ±0.1mm typical; ±0.05mm precision
Roundness of ground shaft: 0.005mm
Cylindricity of bearing seat: 0.008mm

## Chamfer and Fillet Tolerances
Chamfer 1×45°: nominal 1mm at 45°, tolerance ±0.3mm
Radius R2: tolerance ±0.5mm (general); ±0.1mm (precision)

## Title Block / Drawing Standard Notes
Unless otherwise specified (UOS):
- Linear dimensions: ±0.1mm (medium precision), ±0.5mm (general)
- Angular dimensions: ±0.5°
- Surface finish: Ra 3.2 μm
- Break all sharp edges: 0.3 × 45° chamfer
- Material: specify alloy and condition (e.g., EN AW-6082-T6, AISI 1045)
